HOW TO READ THESE NUMBERS
-------------------------
  Reported area is not physics-accepted area. The mask is closed
  morphologically with a 1.0 km element before areas are measured. Closing is
  not wrong — a site has to be a deployable region rather than a scatter of
  pixels — but it moves the number, and at Colca with a 1 km element it
  inflated the reported area by 2.29× against a stride-1 control. Set
  gap_close_km to 0 to see the accepted area directly.

  This run's own funnel puts that factor at 2.10×: closing turned the
  stride-corrected accepted set into a mask that much larger. Divide the
  areas above by it for what the physics accepted.

  Area and capacity are measured on different grids. Area comes from the map
  downsampled by 4, capacity from the full-resolution mask, so a feature a
  few pixels wide loses area while keeping its detectors — around 30% for a
  canyon strip. Use downsample_factor 1 for thin features.

  The scores rank sites against each other for one experiment and one energy
  band. They are not apertures, and no flux or detector response has been
  folded in.

  The layout is anchored, not fitted: detectors are placed from each site's
  bounding-box corner rather than optimised, so capacity is an estimate for
  an arbitrarily placed array.

WHICH OF THESE ARE ASSUMPTIONS
------------------------------
  Choices, not measurements. Check them before quoting a result.

  • geomag_declination_deg = -6.9°
      Does NOT follow the site — it falls back to Arequipa's IGRF value
      wherever the DEM is. Inclination does follow, through a centred dipole.
      Supply the IGRF declination per site.
  • max_range_km = unset
      Column depth accumulates over the whole profile walk, which then stops
      at max_dist_km — so the reported depth is a property of where the walk
      stopped, not of the target's thickness.
  • β, the tau energy-loss constant = 0.6e-6 cm²/g
      Estimated from mass scaling, in the range (0.4–1.0)e-6. Moves the
      production-and-escape optimum in proportion. Not yet pinned to a
      collaboration value.

  Not modelled at all: neutral-current regeneration (so Earth-chord
  suppression is overstated), shower simulation, detector response and
  trigger, and any geology beyond one standard rock density. Nothing here has
  been checked against an external simulation.
