References
==========

The literature this project's numbers rest on. Entries for the two experiments are
taken verbatim from INSPIRE-HEP, so a citation here matches the record a reader will
find there.

Where a figure has been digitized into ``data/``, the file's own header names the
source, the figure number and the DOI — so a curve cannot drift away from the paper it
came from even if it is copied out of the repository.
